Document --validate-checksums

Document the `--validate-checksums` option of `cs bootstrap`, `cs install`, `cs update` and
`cs setup`. On the legacy website: a "Checksums" note on the `bootstrap` page, which explains
the new `bootstrap-jar-sha256` entry, the check at start-up, the error on a mismatch, and
which JARs get no checksum. Also a "Checksums" section on the `install` page (which mentions
that `update` needs the option again), and an "Application checksums" entry in the `setup`
options. On the new website, a "Bootstrap launchers" section on the security page.
